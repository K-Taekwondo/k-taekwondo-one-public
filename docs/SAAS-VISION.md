# Future SaaS direction

## Positioning

A proposed operations workspace for independent Taekwondo studios and small multi-location teams, designed around the relationship between student records, arrival workflows, staff review and studio-owned settings.

## Proposed value

- Reduce repeated administrative steps across student, ID and attendance workflows.
- Keep front-desk check-in focused while staff retain detailed operational context.
- Preserve each studio’s presentation and permission choices as the product grows.

These are product goals, not measured customer outcomes or performance guarantees.

## Business model hypothesis

A subscription per studio, with potential multi-location packaging and optional onboarding assistance, is a direction to evaluate. Pricing, billing implementation, trials, service levels, contracts and support coverage are undecided. No purchase or signup is offered here.

## Conditions before commercialization

Validate customer demand and operating costs; complete release and recovery gates; review tenant isolation, data ownership, retention/export/deletion responsibilities and support processes; establish appropriate legal and privacy terms. No security certification, regulatory compliance certification, guaranteed savings or uptime is claimed.

The public showcase remains separate from private implementation and customer operations. SaaS exploration does not authorize access to or publication of student information.
