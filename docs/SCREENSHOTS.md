## Before publication

1. Use fictional names and data only. Do not reuse student portraits, guardian details, attendance histories, membership documents, or exports.
2. Omit all QR codes and barcodes from public media, including demonstrations.
3. Remove internal addresses, project identifiers, staff accounts, browser chrome, notifications and account menus.
4. Flatten the final image and remove metadata, hidden layers, thumbnails and embedded attachments. Cropping or blurring alone is insufficient.
5. Inspect the exact exported file, including visible text and metadata. Label it “Fictional demo” or “Concept” as appropriate.
6. Publish only the exact reviewed assets under the owner’s authorization. Do not imply a concept image is a shipped feature.

Do not attach private screenshots to issues. If sensitive content is accidentally published, stop further publication and contact the owner privately; deletion alone does not guarantee removal from Git history or copies.
